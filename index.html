Privacy policy

1. Who we are

Minty is an app that helps shop owners in Nigeria keep sales, expenses, debts and receipts. It is made by AY VISION HUB LTD (RC 9876185), House No 12, Minannata Area, Sokoto, Sokoto State, Nigeria. “We” means AY VISION HUB LTD and the Minty team. We are responsible for your information. You can reach us through Support in the app menu, or by email at minty.ng.support@gmail.com.

2. What we keep

The records you type: sales, expenses, debts, receipts, your shop details, and your customers’ names and phone numbers. If you create an account, your phone number and a protected form of your PIN (we never see the PIN itself). If you buy a plan, which plan you have, when it started and ends, and what you paid (from Google Play; never your card details). With Minty Pro, a history of the automatic reminders: who they were for, when, and whether they were sent, failed, or were not sent because your SMS ran out. If you allow notifications, a code from Google that lets us send notifications to your phone, and the app’s language so they come in it. If you send feedback, your message, the phone number you give, and basic app details (language, app version, phone type). On Android, a coded form of your phone’s app ID (not your number, and it can’t be turned back), only to remember when your free days started and which phone your account is open on. If you use “Forgot your PIN?”, your number and a short request code. If the app runs into a problem, a short technical report (what went wrong, app version, phone system), never your records.

3. Where it is kept

Your records are saved on your phone. If you create an account, a copy is backed up on secure servers so you can get it back on a new phone. These servers may be outside Nigeria; we only use providers that protect the information as Nigerian law requires. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone except feedback you choose to send, the coded phone ID above, and short error reports if the app has a problem. Printing a receipt with a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi printer happens between your phone and the printer only.

4. Who helps us

We use a few companies to run Minty, and give each only what it needs for its job: Supabase (our servers and database), Google (Google Play for payments, Firebase for notifications), RevenueCat (keeps track of plans bought on Google Play), and our SMS and WhatsApp providers, such as Africa’s Talking and Meta (WhatsApp), to deliver the reminders you ask for. They may not use your information for anything else.

5. How we use it

Only to run Minty: to show and back up your records, check which plan you have, send the payment reminders you ask for, tell you about your account (for example when your reminder SMS run out) and about Minty, and reply to your feedback. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for adverts.

6. Your customers’ details

Only add customers who agree to it, and only remind people who really owe you. With Minty Pro, a customer’s name, phone number, the amount owed and your shop’s name are passed to our SMS or WhatsApp provider only to deliver the reminder. Reminders go only to Nigerian mobile numbers and end with “-Minty”, so your customer knows where they came from.

7. Payments

Minty and Minty Pro are paid through Google Play; we never see your card details. Minty will never ask for your bank card, BVN or PIN by call or chat.

8. How long we keep it

Your backed-up records and reminder history are kept while you have an account, and deleted with it. A “Forgot your PIN?” request is kept for one day at most. A notification code is removed when you sign out or remove the app. Error reports are deleted after 90 days. Feedback you sent is kept, but no longer linked to you once your account is deleted. Records of payments are kept for our accounts, without your name or number.

9. Keeping it safe

Your information travels to our servers over a protected connection, and each account can see only its own records. Your PIN is never stored as it is, and you can add a fingerprint lock (Menu › Fingerprint lock). Keep your PIN secret: nobody from Minty will ever ask for it.

10. Your choices

You can see, change or delete your records in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything backed up in it, open Account and tap “Delete my account”; it is deleted at once. If you can’t use the app, send a WhatsApp message to Minty Support from the phone number of your account and we will delete it within 30 days. After deletion we keep only a coded form of the account’s phone number (it can’t be turned back into the number), so the free days can’t be started again. You can turn notifications off in your phone’s settings. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023; if you are not happy with how we handle your information, tell us first, and you may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

11. Children

Minty is for shop owners aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep information about children.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will tell you in the app, and the date at the top of this page will change.

Terms of use

1. Using Minty

Minty is provided by AY VISION HUB LTD (RC 9876185), a company registered in Nigeria. By using Minty you agree to these terms. Minty helps you keep records. It is not a bank and it does not hold or move money.

2. Free days and plans

Every new shop gets 14 days of Minty free, with no card needed. When they end, you can still see all your records, but to add or change records you need a plan. Minty and Minty Pro are paid monthly or yearly through Google Play, at the price shown in the app before you pay. A plan renews every month or every year until you cancel it in Google Play; it then stays on until the end of the time you paid for. Moving up to Pro starts at once; moving down, or from yearly to monthly, starts when your paid time ends. Refunds follow Google Play’s rules.

3. Your account

Keep your PIN secret. You are responsible for what is done with your account. One account per phone number. Your account is open on one phone at a time: to use it on another phone, first sign out on the old one. After 5 wrong PINs, sign-in is paused for 24 hours; if you forgot your PIN, use “Forgot your PIN?” to set a new one with Minty Support. Turn on the fingerprint lock (Menu › Fingerprint lock), so that if your phone is lost or stolen nobody can open your records. If that happens, use “Forgot your PIN?” on your new phone: your account moves there and the old phone is signed out.

4. Your records

Your records belong to you. Check important figures yourself: Minty is a tool and does not give financial or tax advice. Without an account, your records are only on your phone, and are lost if the phone is lost or the app is removed.

5. Automatic reminders (Minty Pro)

Minty Pro includes 100 automatic reminders a month, sent by SMS to Nigerian mobile numbers between 9am and 6pm Nigeria time: one the day before a customer’s payment date and one on the day. Unused reminders do not carry over to the next month; extra packs of 100 you buy do not expire. When your reminders run out, no more are sent until you buy more or the next month starts, and we tell you in the app. Each reminder carries your shop’s name and ends with “-Minty”.

6. Reminding customers fairly

Only add customers who agree to it, only remind people who really owe you, and be respectful. Do not use Minty to insult, threaten or harass anyone, or to send anything that is not a real payment reminder. We may stop reminders from, or close, an account that misuses them.

7. Availability

We work to keep Minty running, but it may sometimes be unavailable. Records on your phone keep working without internet. Phone networks may delay or fail to deliver some messages.

8. Responsibility

As far as the law allows, AY VISION HUB LTD is not responsible for losses caused by wrong entries, a lost phone without a backup, or messages that could not be delivered.

9. Ending

You can stop using Minty and delete your account at any time (see the Privacy policy). Cancelling a plan is done in Google Play. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app. If you keep using Minty, you accept the new terms. These terms follow the laws of Nigeria. For questions, use Support in the app menu or email minty.ng.support@gmail.com.
