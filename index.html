Privacy policy

1. Who we are

Minty is an app that helps shop owners in Nigeria keep sales, expenses, debts and receipts. “We” means the Minty team. You can reach us through Support in the app menu.

2. What we keep

The records you type: sales, expenses, debts, receipts, your shop details, and your customers’ names and phone numbers. If you create an account, your phone number and a protected form of your PIN (we never see the PIN itself). If you send feedback, your message, the phone number you give, and basic app details (language, app version, phone type). On Android, a coded form of your phone’s app ID (not your number, and it can’t be turned back), only to remember when your free days started.

3. Where it is kept

Your records are saved on your phone. If you create an account, a copy is backed up on secure servers so you can get it back on a new phone. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone except feedback you choose to send and the coded phone ID above.

4. How we use it

Only to run Minty: to show and back up your records, send the payment reminders you ask for, and reply to your feedback. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for adverts.

5. Your customers’ details

Only add customers who agree to it, and only remind people who really owe you. With Minty Pro, a customer’s name, phone number and the amount owed are passed to our SMS or WhatsApp provider only to deliver the reminder.

6. Payments

Minty Pro is paid through Google Play; we never see your card details. Minty will never ask for your bank card, BVN or PIN by call or chat.

7. Your choices

You can change or delete your records in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything backed up in it, open Account and tap “Delete my account”; it is deleted at once. If you can’t use the app, send a WhatsApp message to Minty Support from the phone number of your account and we will delete it within 30 days. After deletion we keep only a coded form of the account’s phone number (it can’t be turned back into the number), so the free days can’t be started again. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will tell you in the app.

Terms of use

1. Using Minty

By using Minty you agree to these terms. Minty helps you keep records. It is not a bank and it does not hold or move money.

2. Your account

Keep your PIN secret. You are responsible for what is done with your account. One account per phone number.

3. Your records

Your records belong to you. Check important figures yourself: Minty is a tool and does not give financial or tax advice.

4. Reminding customers

Only remind people who really owe you, and be respectful. Do not use Minty to insult, threaten or harass anyone. We may stop reminders from an account that misuses them.

5. Minty Pro

Minty Pro is a monthly subscription through Google Play. It renews every month until you cancel it in Google Play. It includes 100 automatic reminders a month; extra reminder packs do not expire. Refunds follow Google Play’s rules.

6. Availability

We work to keep Minty running, but it may sometimes be unavailable. Records on your phone keep working without internet.

7. Responsibility

As far as the law allows, Minty is not responsible for losses caused by wrong entries, a lost phone without a backup, or messages that could not be delivered.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app. If you keep using Minty, you accept the new terms. These terms follow the laws of Nigeria. For questions, use Support in the app menu.

Delete your Minty account

  1. Open Minty and tap the account button at the top right.
  2. Tap “Delete my account” and confirm.
  3. Your account and every record backed up in it are deleted at once, and removed from that phone.

Can’t open the app? Send us a WhatsApp message from the phone number of your account, saying you want it deleted. We delete the account and all its records within 30 days and tell you when it is done. Feedback you sent us is kept without your account. If you pay for a plan, also cancel it in Google Play.
